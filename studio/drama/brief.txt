A 2-minute comedic drama with anthropomorphic fruit characters in a cozy kitchen at night. Mango, a proud, dramatic mango, and Pineapple, a grumpy spiky pineapple, argue over who gets to be the star of tomorrow's fruit salad, while Little Lime, a nervous young lime, tries to keep the peace. Clear dialogue, a small slapstick fight on the kitchen counter (pushing, rolling, a cherry thrown), and a warm funny ending where they agree to share the spotlight.
